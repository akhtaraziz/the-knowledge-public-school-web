About Our School

Our Mission & Vision

The Knowledge Public School, Multan, is committed to providing holistic education that fosters intellectual growth, moral values, and social responsibility.

Established in 2005, our school has been a beacon of quality education in Multan, preparing students to excel in academics and life.

  • Qualified & Experienced Faculty
  • Student-Centered Learning Approach
  • Focus on Character Building
  • Modern Teaching Methodologies

Admission Process

  1. Inquiry: Visit campus or call for information
  2. Submission: Complete admission form with required documents
  3. Assessment: Student interaction/assessment (if required)
  4. Confirmation: Fee submission and enrollment

Required Documents

  • Birth Certificate (Original + 2 copies)
  • Previous School Report Card (if applicable)
  • B-Form/CNIC of Student
  • 4 Passport-size Photographs
  • Parents' CNIC Copies
