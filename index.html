About Our School

A Legacy of Excellence in Education

Our Mission & Vision

The Knowledge Public School, Taha Campus Multan, is committed to providing holistic education that fosters intellectual growth, moral values, and social responsibility.

Established in 1995, our school has been a beacon of quality education in Multan, preparing students to excel in academics and life. Our black and gold colors symbolize excellence, wisdom, and the pursuit of knowledge.

Admission Process

  1. Inquiry: Visit campus or call for information
  2. Submission: Complete admission form with required documents
  3. Assessment: Student interaction/assessment (if required)
  4. Confirmation: Fee submission and enrollment

Required Documents

  • Birth Certificate (Original + 2 copies)
  • Previous School Report Card (if applicable)
  • B-Form/CNIC of Student
  • 4 Passport-size Photographs
  • Parents' CNIC Copies
